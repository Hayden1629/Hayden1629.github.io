UMN Biotech & Finance Club

cross-disciplinary student group focused on biotech and capital markets

about this page

Biotech and Finance club was founded by Hayden Herstrom in collaboration with Andrew Peterson and Henry Likness. The club originated out of the joint analysis of biotech company Anavex Life Sciences and the misadventures of their drug, blarcamesine. We realized there was no club on campus for pharmaceutical research with the end goal of taking positions in financial markets, so we decided to start the club.

In addition to investigating biotech candidate drugs, we also hosted speakers, visited local companies, and gave presentations on things that interest us.
